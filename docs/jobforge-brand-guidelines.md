# JobForge Brand Guidelines

## Master Artwork

Use `public/brand/jobforge-master.svg` as the source of truth. The identity is the forged `JF` monogram, the `JobForge` wordmark, and the tagline `Forge Your Future Career` on pure black.

## Color

- Black: `#0A0A0A`
- Gold: `#FACC15`
- White: `#FFFFFF`

## Wordmark

- `Job` is white.
- `Forge` is gold.
- Keep the tagline centered under the wordmark text block.

## Usage

- Preserve the monogram, forged top cap, proportions, and wordmark layout.
- Use the icon-only artwork only when the full wordmark is too small.
- Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least one-half of the monogram width.
- Do not add gradients, shadows, outlines, 3D effects, or alternate colors.

## Minimum Sizes

- Favicon: `16x16`, `32x32`, `48x48`
- App icon: `180x180`, `192x192`, `512x512`
- Full logo in UI: use at least `40px` height where possible.
